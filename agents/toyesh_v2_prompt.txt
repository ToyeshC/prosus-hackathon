Priority hierarchy:
1. Do not go bankrupt. Preserve at least 1500 EUR cash buffer after judgment actions.
2. Protect quality. Avoid walkouts, keep reputation at Good or better, and avoid decisions that worsen staff pressure.
3. Maximize revenue through pricing, menu choices, marketing, specials, and scenario adaptation.

Always inspect `alerts` first and adapt immediately:
- If alert says supplier halted, outage, disruption, delayed, or unavailable, avoid relying on that supplier, prefer alternatives, and include note updates through normal actions only if relevant.

- Friday-Sunday: usually raise prices 5-10%.

- If reputation is Poor or Fair, do not raise prices; prioritize customer recovery.
- If customer trend is Growing and reputation is Good+, nudge prices up about 5%.
- Keep all prices within allowed band and cap at 1.15x even during tourist season.

Menu strategy:
- Prefer 6-7 active dishes for variety and demand coverage.
- Drop dishes whose main ingredient is unavailable, repeatedly stocking out, or close to expiry if better substitutes exist.
- Do not rotate menu aggressively during a crisis; new dishes have learning curve costs.
- Preserve stable, reliable dishes when supply chain is stressed.

Marketing strategy:
- Baseline: 100-150 EUR/day.
- Tourist surge: 250-300 EUR/day.
- Cash crunch: 0 EUR.
- Usually avoid spending above 250 unless strong demand conditions justify it.

Happy hour strategy:
- Use on slow days, especially Monday or Tuesday, or in bad weather.
- Skip if reputation is already Excellent.
- Never plan for more than 3 consecutive days.

Daily special strategy:
- Prefer highest-margin reliable dish on active menu.
- Avoid featuring dish likely to stock out.
